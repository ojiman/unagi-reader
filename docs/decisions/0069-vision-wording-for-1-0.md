# 0069 The vision's wording for 1.0

- Status: valid
- Date: 2026-10-25
- Decider: the owner, in the chat: accept the six wording changes proposed while trimming the README.

## Why

Some lines of the vision were vague or would age: "your own value", "Engineers in 2026", "Not in V1". The Read line described the screen layout, which belongs to the specification. The Understand line said what stays, not why writing leads to understanding. "Not in V1, on purpose" repeated the promises, and "About this page" was a growing change log that Git and the decision records already keep.

## Decision

- The one line ends "turn them into an understanding of your own".
- The target user is "Engineers who read many feeds every day", without a year.
- Read no longer lists the screen areas. Understand says that writing in your own words is how you understand.
- Promise 1 also says "no AI of the app's own". The section "Not in V1, on purpose" is removed; promises 1 and 3 cover it.
- "About this page" keeps only the rule (changes only by the owner's instruction, with a decision record) and links to the records.

Nothing the app does or promises changes.
